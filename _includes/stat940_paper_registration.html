Presentation rubric

The presentation is worth 10% of the final course grade. The weights below apply to the presentation mark.

CriterionWhat we assessWeight
Motivation & problemExplain why the work matters and clearly define the problem it addresses.20%
Main idea & technical understandingExplain how the approach works, with enough technical depth to demonstrate understanding. Establish the main ideas before finer details.35%
Findings & interpretationExplain the main results, what we learn from them, and relevant limitations.20%
Clarity & organizationTeach the essential ideas clearly and selectively. Plan approximately 15–20 minutes of prepared material within the 25-minute session.15%
Discussion & questionsAllow approximately 5–10 minutes for discussion. Invite questions throughout and respond thoughtfully, demonstrating understanding.10%

You are assessed on understanding and explanation, not on covering every detail. Discussion is assessed by how you facilitate and respond to questions, not by how many questions classmates ask.
