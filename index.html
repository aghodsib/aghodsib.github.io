Ali Ghodsi is a Professor at the University of Waterloo, Director of the Data Science Lab, and a Faculty Affiliate at the Vector Institute. His research focuses on developing new methods and algorithms in machine learning and artificial intelligence, with particular interests in deep learning, large language models, and AI for scientific and mathematical discovery. His work explores how advances in learning algorithms can help address open problems in science, mathematics, and statistics.

He is the co-author of Elements of Deep Learning (Springer, 2026) and Elements of Dimensionality Reduction and Manifold Learning (Springer, 2024). Through his books and openly available university lectures, he also works to make advanced ideas in machine learning accessible to students, researchers, and practitioners.

Data Science Lab

The Data Science Lab brings together students and researchers developing new methods and algorithms in machine learning and artificial intelligence. The lab's current interests include deep learning, large language models, and AI for scientific and mathematical discovery, particularly approaches that can help address open problems in science, mathematics, and statistics.
